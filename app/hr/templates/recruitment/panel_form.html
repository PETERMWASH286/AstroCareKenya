Interview panels should include diverse stakeholders from different departments for comprehensive evaluation.

Panel Types

Technical Panel
Assesses technical skills and expertise. Include senior engineers or subject matter experts.
HR Panel
Evaluates cultural fit and soft skills. Typically includes HR managers.
Management Panel
Final decision-making panel with department heads and executives.
Final Panel
Comprehensive evaluation combining all aspects before job offer.

Best Practices

  • Include 3-5 members for optimal discussion
  • Assign a Chairperson to lead discussions
  • Include members from different seniority levels
  • Schedule availability checks before finalizing
