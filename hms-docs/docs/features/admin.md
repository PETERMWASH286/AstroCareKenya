# Administration & Multi-Hospital Management — AstroCare

AstroCare’s **Administration Module** provides a modern, centralized control center for hospital and system administrators. Whether you are managing a single hospital or multiple facilities as a Super Admin, this module ensures **full oversight, operational control, and compliance**.

---

## 🏥 Hospital Admin Features

### **1. Facility & Hospital Profile Management**
- Maintain comprehensive hospital profiles: name, address, contacts, branding, time zones, and service configurations.
- Update department details, services offered, and operational settings.
- Manage hospital resource centers (e.g., Pharmacy, Lab, Radiology, Medical Records) efficiently from one dashboard.

### **2. Reports & Audit Logs**
- Access real-time reports from all departments: Patients, Billing, Pharmacy, Laboratory, Radiology, HR, Finance, and Inventory.
- View detailed **audit logs** tracking all user actions for compliance and security reviews.
- Export reports for meetings, board reviews, or regulatory compliance.

### **3. Board Member & Communication Module**
- Create and manage **board member profiles**.
- Post announcements, circulars, and critical communications to staff or departments.
- Receive notifications when new posts are created, approved, or require review.

### **4. Notifications & Alerts**
- Stay informed with **system notifications** for key events:
  - Critical lab results
  - Payment or billing approvals
  - Resource stock alerts
  - Compliance checks and audit reminders
- Customize notification preferences per admin or role.

### **5. System Maintenance & Security**
- Perform **system backups** for disaster recovery.
- Monitor and manage resource centers, including storage, equipment, and critical assets.
- Implement role-based access and compliance checks in accordance with Health Act 2017 and Data Protection Act 2019.

---

## 🌐 Super Admin Features (Multi-Hospital Oversight)

### **1. Unified Dashboard**
- Access **all hospitals** under a single super admin dashboard.
- View aggregated reports, analytics, and performance metrics across facilities.
- Manage inter-hospital communications and policies from one place.

### **2. Hospital Management**
- Add new hospitals to the network with full setup guidance.
- Configure departments, resource centers, and payment integrations for each facility.
- Set super admin roles, delegate permissions, and manage hospital hierarchies.

### **3. Advanced Reporting**
- Generate cross-hospital analytics: patient volumes, financial performance, service utilization, and staff efficiency.
- Track compliance, audit logs, and critical operational alerts for all managed hospitals.

### **4. Notifications & Workflow Oversight**
- Receive alerts for events across all hospitals:
  - Service requests
  - Resource shortages
  - Critical incidents
  - Policy approvals
- Streamline review and approval workflows to reduce delays.

---

## 🛠 Workflow Examples

1. **Adding a New Hospital**
   - Navigate to **Super Admin → Add Hospital**.
   - Fill in hospital details, configure departments, services, and branding.
   - Assign admin and board members to the facility.

2. **Managing Reports**
   - Access **Reports & Analytics → Department Reports**.
   - Filter by hospital, department, or time range.
   - Export reports in PDF or Excel for board meetings.

3. **Audit & Compliance**
   - Review detailed user activity logs.
   - Ensure all sensitive actions are tracked for compliance purposes.

4. **Resource Center Management**
   - Monitor stock, lab equipment, or radiology devices.
   - Approve departmental requests for resources.
   - Track maintenance schedules and asset utilization.
