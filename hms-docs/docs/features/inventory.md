# Inventory Management — AstroCare

AstroCare’s **Inventory Module** is the central hub for managing hospital stores and warehouses. It empowers store managers, procurement officers, and department heads to **track stock, handle orders, manage suppliers, and maintain full control of medical and non-medical supplies**.  

The module is designed for **accuracy, efficiency, and transparency**, ensuring that every item—from medicines to medical equipment—is accounted for and readily available when needed.

---

## 🚀 Key Features Overview

- **Stock Management**
  - Track all items in the store: medication, consumables, medical devices, and assets.
  - Automatic notifications for **expiry dates**, low stock alerts, and reorder thresholds.
  - View real-time inventory levels per item and department.

- **Order Management**
  - Departments can request items through the system.
  - Store managers review, approve, and dispatch requested items.
  - Maintain **delivery logs** and track orders from request to fulfillment.

- **Supplier Management**
  - Add, update, and manage supplier information.
  - Link suppliers to specific categories (medications, lab reagents, medical equipment).
  - Track supplier performance and history of orders.

- **Purchase Orders**
  - Create new purchase orders for suppliers.
  - Track pending, approved, and delivered orders.
  - Record invoices and attach supporting documents.
  - Sync orders with financial module for payment tracking.

- **Expiry & Quality Control**
  - Monitor expiration dates for medications and consumables.
  - Generate reports of items near expiry and take proactive action.
  - Conduct quality checks and manage defective or returned items.

- **Asset & Equipment Tracking**
  - Track usage, maintenance, and location of medical equipment.
  - Schedule routine maintenance and log equipment downtime.
  - Link equipment to relevant departments for accountability.

- **Inventory Reports & Analytics**
  - Real-time reports of stock levels, order fulfillment, supplier performance, and asset usage.
  - Exportable reports for auditing and strategic planning.

---

## Inventory Workflow

1. **Adding Items to Inventory**
   - Navigate to **Inventory → Add New Item**.
   - Provide details: Item name, category, supplier, unit price, quantity, expiry date (if applicable), and storage location.
   - Images can be uploaded for easier identification.

2. **Receiving Orders**
   - Record deliveries from suppliers.
   - Verify quantity, batch, and expiry before approving stock.
   - Mark items as available in the system.

3. **Department Requests**
   - Departments submit item requests via the system.
   - Store manager reviews and approves requests.
   - Items are delivered to the department with a **delivery note**.

4. **Stock Monitoring**
   - Automated alerts for low stock, expired items, and nearing expiry.
   - Track stock movement to prevent discrepancies.
   - Reports available for daily, weekly, and monthly inventory audit.

5. **Supplier Orders**
   - Create purchase orders for needed items.
   - Track order status: Pending, Approved, Shipped, Received.
   - Update inventory automatically once items are received.

---

## Advanced Features

- **Integration with Finance**
  - Purchase orders and deliveries automatically reflect in the **Finance Module** for payment tracking.
  - Cost of goods updates hospital ledgers in real-time.

- **Integration with Pharmacy & Lab**
  - Automatic inventory decrement when items are used in pharmacy or laboratory.
  - Track consumption per department for analytics and planning.

- **Barcode & QR Code Support**
  - Scan items during receipt, issue, or stocktaking.
  - Reduces errors and improves efficiency.

- **Audit Trail & Logs**
  - Every inventory transaction is logged: item, quantity, user, timestamp, and action.
  - Ensures transparency and compliance for audits.

---

## Recommended Practices

- Regularly update stock levels and expiry dates.
- Approve orders promptly to avoid delays in patient care.
- Conduct periodic audits to reconcile physical and system stock.
- Ensure all suppliers are vetted and linked correctly to categories.
