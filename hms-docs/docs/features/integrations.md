# Integrations — AstroCare

AstroCare is designed to **interoperate seamlessly** with national, regional, and global systems, making hospital operations smarter, faster, and more connected. Our integrations ensure **data flows securely**, services are automated, and staff can focus on patient care.

---

## 🌐 Digital Health Superhighway (Kenya)

AstroCare connects directly with the **Digital Health Superhighway (DHS) of Kenya**, enabling:

- **Patient Data Exchange**: Access or share patient records across hospitals securely.
- **eClaims**: Submit insurance claims electronically for faster approvals.
- **Eligibility Checks**: Verify patient insurance and benefits in real-time.
- **Worker Registry Integration**: Register or verify healthcare workers.
- **Other National Services**: Health IDs, immunization registries, lab results, and more.

> Access is always **secure, encrypted, and patient-consent driven** using OTP verification workflows.

Learn more: [Digital Health Superhighway Kenya](https://digitalhealth.go.ke/)

---

## 💬 Bulk SMS Integration — Africa’s Talking

- Send appointment reminders, lab results, billing notifications, and emergency alerts via **bulk SMS**.
- Staff notifications for schedules, duty rotations, and critical events.
- Customizable SMS templates for patients and employees.
- Delivery reports for accountability.

Learn more: [Africa’s Talking Bulk SMS](https://africastalking.com/)

---

## 🧬 Clinical Integrations

1. **Diagnosis & Coding**
   - Integrated with **WHO ICD-11 API** for standardized diagnostic codes.
   - Automatic coding suggestions for doctors based on symptoms and patient history.

2. **Laboratory Tests**
   - **LOINC FHIR API** integration for lab tests standardization.
   - Supports lab test codes, parameter ranges, and interoperability with other labs.

---

## 📧 Email & Notification Integrations

- Automated email notifications for:
  - Appointment confirmations
  - Lab and radiology results
  - Billing and invoices
  - Internal HR or administrative announcements
- Integration with SMTP providers or hospital mail servers.
- Alerts are logged in system activity for audit purposes.

---

## 💳 Payment & Financial Integrations

- **M-Pesa STK Push / PayBill**
  - Patients can pay for consultations, lab tests, pharmacy, or other hospital services **before service delivery**.
- Optional integration with other local and international payment gateways.
- Automated reconciliation with **Finance module**.

Learn more: [M-Pesa for Business](https://www.safaricom.co.ke/personal/m-pesa)

---

## ⚙️ Other Integrations

- **Insurance APIs**: Verify patient coverage, submit claims, and receive updates in real-time.
- **Third-Party Scheduling**: Sync calendars and appointments with external systems.
- **Hospital Equipment IoT Integration**: Track devices, lab equipment status, and usage.
- **Analytics Platforms**: Export anonymized data for BI dashboards and AI analytics.

---

## 📌 Key Benefits of AstroCare Integrations

- Reduces manual work and human errors.
- Improves speed of claims, lab results, and patient communication.
- Ensures **regulatory compliance** and audit trails.
- Streamlines hospital operations from a single dashboard.
