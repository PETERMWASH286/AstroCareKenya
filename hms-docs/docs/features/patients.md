# Patient Management — AstroCare

Managing patient records in AstroCare is designed to be simple, secure, and fast. This guide walks you through creating, searching, and maintaining patient records with best practices.

---

## Overview

AstroCare provides a **centralized patient profile** for every individual, accessible through the HMS dashboard. Each profile includes demographic details, medical history, visit records, and billing information.  

You can also **search patients using the Digital Health Superhighway** (DHSH) for interoperability across multiple hospitals.

---

## Key Features

- **Create new patient profiles** quickly with all essential details
- **Advanced search** by name, ID, phone, or Digital Health Superhighway ID
- **View complete patient history**: visits, diagnoses, prescriptions, lab results
- **Attach documents and images** (X-rays, reports, scanned IDs)
- **Secure editing and audit logging** for every change
- **Merge duplicate records** to maintain clean data

---

## Adding a New Patient

1. Go to **Patients → Add New** in the sidebar.
2. Enter:
   - **Basic Info:** Full name, gender, date of birth, ID/passport
   - **Contact:** Phone number, email, address
   - **Emergency Contact:** Name and phone
   - **Insurance details** (optional)
3. Upload any supporting documents (e.g., ID, insurance card).
4. Click **Save & Continue**.

**Quick Tip:** Fields marked `*` are mandatory. Use accurate data to avoid duplicates.

## Searching for a Patient

Use the **search bar** at the top of the Patients module to quickly find patients by:
- **Name or partial name**
- **Phone number**
- **National ID / Passport**
- **Digital Health Superhighway ID** (for cross-hospital access)

> If connected to DHSH, you can **query external hospitals** (with consent) to retrieve verified patient records.

## Viewing & Managing Patient Profile

Click a patient name to open the **Patient Dashboard**:
- **Demographics & contacts**
- **Visit history** (OPD/IPD)
- **Lab & radiology reports**
- **Prescriptions & medications**
- **Billing & payment history**
- **Attachments** (ID copies, reports, images)

**Actions available:**
- Update patient details (with audit tracking)
- Add new visit or appointment
- Upload medical documents
- Print patient summary or ID card

---

## Dealing with Duplicates

If duplicates exist:
1. Select the records to merge.
2. Choose the primary record to retain.
3. Merge — all visits and history will combine under the primary profile.

---

## Integration with Digital Health Superhighway

AstroCare connects to the DHSH network for **inter-hospital patient lookup**:
- Verify patient identity during referrals.
- Access shared medical history (subject to consent).
- Improve continuity of care across facilities.

**Setup required:** Ensure DHSH integration is enabled in **Administration → Integrations**.

---

## Best Practices
- Always confirm **patient identity** before creating a new record.
- Use **search first** to avoid duplicates.
- Keep **contact and insurance info updated**.
- Use **secure upload** for sensitive documents.
- Regularly review and clean duplicate data.
