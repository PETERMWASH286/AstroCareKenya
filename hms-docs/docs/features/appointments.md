# Appointment Management — AstroCare

Efficient scheduling is at the heart of patient care. AstroCare offers a **modern appointment management system** designed to minimize wait times, optimize doctor availability, and provide a seamless experience for both staff and patients.

---

## Why Appointments Matter
A well-managed appointment system:
- Reduces patient waiting time
- Improves staff utilization
- Enhances patient satisfaction
- Prevents scheduling conflicts

---

## Booking an Appointment (After Patient Registration)

Once a patient is registered, you can book an appointment in just a few clicks.

### **Steps to Book an Appointment**
1. Navigate to **Appointments → New Booking**.
2. Select the **Patient** (search by name, ID, or phone number).
3. Choose the **Visit Type**:
   - General Consultation
   - Dental Clinic
   - Optician Clinic
   - Special Clinics
   - Walk-in (Lab)
   - Walk-in (Radiology)
   - Walk-in (Pharmacy)
4. Assign the **Doctor or Provider**:
   - Filter by specialty (e.g., Dentist, Optometrist)
   - View available slots in real time
5. Set the **Date & Time**:
   - View availability using the calendar or timeline view
6. Confirm and **Save Appointment**.

## Real-Time Doctor Availability

AstroCare shows **live schedules for each doctor**:
- **Color-coded slots** for available, booked, and blocked times
- **Instant conflict detection** (prevents double booking)
- **Custom scheduling rules** (break times, maximum patients per day)

---

## Appointment Types Explained

| Visit Type             | Description                                                       |
|------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| General Consultation    | Standard outpatient visit with a general doctor                 |
| Dental Clinic           | Visit for dental procedures and consultations                   |
| Optician Clinic         | Eye checkups and vision care                                    |
| Special Clinics         | Specialty consultations (e.g., cardiology, pediatrics)          |
| Walk-in (Lab)           | Direct lab test without prior consultation                      |
| Walk-in (Radiology)     | Direct imaging (X-ray, CT scan, MRI)                            |
| Walk-in (Pharmacy)      | Direct medication dispensing                                    |

## How Doctors View Their Appointments

Doctors have access to **My Schedule** under their dashboard:
- **Daily, weekly, or monthly calendar views**
- **Patient details** for each booking
- Ability to **accept, reschedule, or cancel** appointments
- **Integrated notifications**: Email & SMS reminders to patients and doctors

**Pro Tip:** Doctors can also block their availability for meetings, surgeries, or off-days.

## Managing Reschedules & Cancellations
- Staff can **reschedule** an appointment by dragging and dropping on the calendar.
- Patients receive **automatic SMS/email alerts** for changes.
- Cancellations are logged for **audit tracking**.

---

## Integration with Other Features
- **Billing**: Option to pre-authorize payment during booking.
- **Notifications**: Automatic reminders to reduce no-shows.
- **Reports**: Track doctor utilization, appointment trends, and cancellations.

---

## Best Practices
- Always **verify patient information** before booking.
- Use **calendar filters** to avoid overlapping appointments.
- Enable **reminder notifications** for both doctors and patients.
- Keep **doctor availability updated** to prevent conflicts.
