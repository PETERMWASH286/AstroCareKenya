# Human Resources (HR) Management — AstroCare

AstroCare’s **HR Module** is your centralized hub to manage hospital staff efficiently. From onboarding new employees to tracking attendance, payroll, and disciplinary actions, this module ensures that **staff management is transparent, compliant, and streamlined**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features Overview

- **Staff Profiles & Biographies**
  - Maintain detailed staff profiles: name, photo, role, department, qualifications, certifications, and emergency contacts.
  - Track employment history, promotions, and department transfers.
  - Link staff profiles to payroll, attendance, and duties.

- **Attendance & Duty Tracking**
  - Record daily attendance automatically via biometric systems or manual entry.
  - Track shifts, on-call schedules, and overtime.
  - Generate attendance reports for payroll and performance review.

- **Payroll Management**
  - Automate salary calculation including basic pay, allowances, deductions, and bonuses.
  - Generate payslips and tax forms.
  - Integrates with financial module for real-time accounting and expense tracking.
  - Support multiple pay frequencies: monthly, bi-weekly, or custom cycles.

- **Disciplinary Management**
  - Document disciplinary cases, warnings, and resolutions.
  - Track actions taken and generate reports for HR audits.
  - Maintain confidentiality and compliance with labor regulations.

- **Recruitment & Onboarding**
  - Manage job postings, applications, and interview scheduling.
  - Track recruitment pipeline and hire status.
  - Automatically onboard new employees into HR, payroll, and department systems.

- **Leave & Benefits Management**
  - Configure leave types (annual, sick, maternity/paternity, etc.) and approval workflows.
  - Track leave balances, approvals, and history.
  - Manage benefits including health insurance, retirement contributions, and allowances.

- **Performance Management**
  - Track key performance indicators (KPIs) for staff evaluations.
  - Schedule performance reviews and generate reports.
  - Link outcomes to promotions, salary adjustments, and training needs.

- **Audit & Compliance**
  - Complete audit trail for staff management actions.
  - Ensure compliance with labor laws, Health Act 2017, and Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.
  - Export reports for HR compliance and management review.

## HR Workflow Examples

1. **Adding New Staff**
   - Navigate to **HR → Add New Staff**.
   - Fill in personal information, role, department, and employment start date.
   - Assign system roles and connect payroll account.

2. **Recording Attendance**
   - Biometric or manual entry options.
   - Review daily, weekly, or monthly attendance dashboards.
   - Highlight anomalies or absent staff automatically.

3. **Payroll Processing**
   - Calculate salaries based on attendance and pre-defined rules.
   - Approve payroll and send electronic payslips.
   - Integrate with financial module for expense tracking and reporting.

4. **Disciplinary Actions**
   - Document cases including date, staff involved, and actions taken.
   - Generate summary reports for management review.

5. **Recruitment & Onboarding**
   - Post open positions, manage applications, and track candidate progress.
   - Onboard new hires directly into HR and payroll systems.
