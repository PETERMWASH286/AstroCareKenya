## 📞 Support Channels

### 1. Email Support
Reach out to our support team directly via email. Include relevant details such as **hospital name, user role, issue description, and screenshots** for faster assistance.  

- **General Support:** support@astrocare.com  
- **Technical & Integration Support:** techsupport@astrocare.com  
- **Billing & Finance Support:** finance@astrocare.com  

> 💡 **Tip:** Always include your **user ID, patient ID (if applicable), and timestamps** for the issue.

## 📝 What Information to Provide

When contacting support, please include:  

1. **Your Role & Department** – (e.g., Super Admin, Nurse, Lab Technician)  
2. **Hospital/Facility Name & Subdomain**  
3. **Issue Description** – Detailed steps to reproduce the issue.  
4. **Screenshots / Screen Recording** – Helps us understand and resolve faster.  
5. **Date & Time of Occurrence**  
6. **Any Error Codes or Logs** – Especially for payment, integration, or system errors.

> Providing these details ensures **rapid and precise solutions**.
