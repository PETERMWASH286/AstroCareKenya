    AstroCare — Hospital Management System (HMS)¶

    A modern, secure, and cloud-first HMS built to streamline operations, improve clinical workflows, and deliver better patient experiences across single and multi-facility networks.

    🚀 Get started — astrocarekenya.com

    What is AstroCare?¶

    AstroCare is the hospital’s digital command center. It brings together patient management, clinical workflows, labs, radiology, pharmacy, billing, HR, inventory and finance — all from a single, easy-to-use web interface. Designed for hospitals that need accuracy, security, and scale.

    Core capabilities (quick overview)¶

    • Patient Management — unified patient profiles, DHSH-powered cross-facility search, merge & dedupe.
    • Appointments & Queues — fast booking, walk-ins, clinic types, and doctor schedules.
    • Laboratory & Radiology — sample tracking, result entry, LOINC & FHIR support, QC, blood bank.
    • Pharmacy & Inventory — prescription flows, stock control, expiry alerts, purchase orders.
    • Billing & Payments — invoices, cash, insurance workflows, and M-Pesa (STK Push & Paybill) integration.
    • Finance & Reporting — pricing, receivables, supplier payments, P&L and analytics.
    • HR & Payroll — staff bios, attendance, duty rosters, recruitment and payroll.
    • Admin & Multi-Hospital — facility configuration, board communications, backups, audit logs.
    • Security & Compliance — role-based access, 2FA (SMS/email), encryption in transit & at rest, audit trails (Health Act 2017 & DPA 2019).
    • Integrations — Digital Health Superhighway (Kenya), Africa’s Talking (SMS), WHO ICD-11, email, payment gateways, FHIR APIs.

    Why hospitals choose AstroCare¶

    • Single dashboard for many hospitals — Super Admin manages multiple facilities, policies and consolidated reports.
    • Pre-service payment enforcement — ensures lab and pharmacy services are settled before processing.
    • Standards-first interoperability — LOINC, FHIR and national DHSH compatibility for secure, consented data exchange.
    • Built for compliance — audit trails, encryption, data minimization and patient consent flows (OTP based) for cross-facility access.
    • Operational resilience — backups, monitoring, and role-based escalation notifications.

    Quick start — two short paths¶

    For IT / Hospital Admin (get live)¶

    1. Request an account at: https://astrocarekenya.com/get-started
    2. Create Super Admin and add facility details (name, timezone, branding).
    3. Configure payment (M-Pesa credentials / Paybill), SMTP, and SMS provider (Africa’s Talking).
    4. Load core data: departments, tariffs, services, and staff.
    5. Run the Go-Live checklist (see Getting Started → First-Time Checklist).

    For Clinicians & Front-line Staff¶

    1. Open your hospital subdomain (example):
      https://<hospital-shortname>.astrocarekenya.com
    2. Use the global search (/) to find patient, appointment or order.
    3. Start consultations from the Queue, place orders (labs/rad/pharmacy), and sign off results.
    4. Enable 2FA and review your My Schedule dashboard daily.

    Security & Patient Consent¶

    • Passwords hashed with modern algorithms; 2FA available (SMS or email OTP).
    • Patient consent is required for DHSH cross-facility lookups — an OTP is sent to the patient for approval before data is shared.
    • Encryption: TLS for transport; AES-256 (or equivalent) at rest.
    • Auditability: full logs of who accessed or changed clinical/financial records.

    Integrations that matter¶

    • Digital Health Superhighway (Kenya) — cross-facility record lookup, eClaims, worker registry.
    • Africa’s Talking — bulk SMS for appointment reminders and result notifications.
    • M-Pesa (Daraja API) — STK Push & Paybill for instant payments.
    • LOINC & FHIR — standardized lab codes and Observation/DiagnosticReport exchange.
    • WHO ICD-11 API — diagnostic coding suggestions.
