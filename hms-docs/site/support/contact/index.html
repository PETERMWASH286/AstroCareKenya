    Contact & Support — AstroCare¶

    Welcome to the AstroCare Contact Page!
    Here you can find all the ways to get professional, fast, and friendly support for your hospital management system. Whether you are a super admin, hospital staff, or IT partner, we are here to ensure your operations run smoothly.

    📞 Support Channels¶

    1. Email Support¶

    Reach out to our support team directly via email. Include relevant details such as hospital name, user role, issue description, and screenshots for faster assistance.

    • General Support: support@astrocare.com
    • Technical & Integration Support: techsupport@astrocare.com
    • Billing & Finance Support: finance@astrocare.com

    💡 Tip: Always include your user ID, patient ID (if applicable), and timestamps for the issue.

    2. Phone Support¶

    Speak directly with our support specialists.
    - Kenya: +254 700 123 456
    - International: +254 700 123 456
    - Available Monday – Friday, 8 AM – 6 PM (EAT)

    3. Live Chat¶

    For quick queries and guidance, use our in-app live chat:
    - Navigate to Help → Live Chat in your AstroCare dashboard.
    - Real-time support from our trained support agents.

    4. Community Forum¶

    Connect with other AstroCare users, share experiences, and find solutions:
    AstroCare Discussions

    • Search for similar issues before posting to get instant answers.
    • Upvote and comment on helpful threads to improve community knowledge.

    5. Remote Assistance¶

    For complex technical issues, our team can remotely access your system (with proper authorization) to troubleshoot:
    - Requires prior scheduling via support email or phone.
    - Secure remote session using encrypted connection.

    📝 What Information to Provide¶

    When contacting support, please include:

    1. Your Role & Department – (e.g., Super Admin, Nurse, Lab Technician)
    2. Hospital/Facility Name & Subdomain
    3. Issue Description – Detailed steps to reproduce the issue.
    4. Screenshots / Screen Recording – Helps us understand and resolve faster.
    5. Date & Time of Occurrence
    6. Any Error Codes or Logs – Especially for payment, integration, or system errors.

    Providing these details ensures rapid and precise solutions.

    ⏱ Expected Response Times¶

    Channel Response Time
    Email Support Within 24 hours
    Phone Support Immediate (during office hours)
    Live Chat Within 10 minutes
    Remote Assistance By appointment

    High-priority issues (e.g., system downtime, payment failures) are escalated immediately.

    🛡 Security & Privacy Note¶

    All support communications are secure and confidential:
    - Personal or patient data is never shared externally.
    - Remote sessions use encrypted connections.
    - Sensitive requests (e.g., patient data access) require proper authorization and consent.
